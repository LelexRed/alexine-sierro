Crush

3D graphics - video

2023

Proposed video clip for the artists Yakary and Ehro. The song presents a broken-hearted individual trying to emancipate himself from these negative emotions by losing himself in music.

The project alternates between nostalgic 3D and shots filmed in the clouds, reflecting a fracture between the real world and the mental universe in which the protagonist takes refuge.

made in collaboration with Matteo Corthesi
