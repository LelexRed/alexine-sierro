tide against tide

3D graphics - installation

2025

The interactive installation Tide Agains tide explores the theme of push-back incidents in the context of the migration crisis in the Mediterranean. It follows an incident documented by journalist Bashar Deep, with scenes from the group appearing as clouds of dots.

Users can choose the sound channel they want (factual, legal, geographical or meteorological), determining the degree of neutrality with which they perceive the story. Image and sound evolve over distinct temporalities, offering a unique combination for each interaction.
