Corpuscule

3D graphics - vr

2023

Corpuscule is a project made up of photogrametric data points from the British Museum. These particles, which make up the image of the museum, are manipulated and distorted throughout the sequence, distorting its initial state. This fragmentation of particles echoes accusations of artefact theft by museum institutions, in this case the British Museum.

The sequence was created for a 360° silo projection and also adapted into a VR sequence.
