Shared goals shared future

3D vr sculpt - visual identity

2024

Shared Goals, Shared Future is a multimedia project designed to raise awareness of the Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs) among the younger generation through engaging and accessible media.

Comprising a printed poster, five animated videos and an Instagram account, the project uses virtual reality worlds to illustrate key issues such as access to clean water, the protection of biodiversity on land and in water, and renewable energy in an organic and immersive way.

made in collaboration with Coraline Beyler, Flora Hayoz and Delphine Brantschen
