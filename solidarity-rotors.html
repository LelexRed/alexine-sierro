Solidarity Rotors

website

2023

Solidarity Rotors imagines an alternative world where cars have given way to helicopters as the main means of transport. This speculative project takes the form of a fictitious website designed as a communication tool for a committed political party.

The party is campaigning for universal and equitable access to air transport, promoting the idea of collective air mobility based on solidarity and accessible to all.
