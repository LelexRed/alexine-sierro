Remind me i'm alive

electronics

2024

"Remind me i'm alive" is a hybrid companion object, part fidget toy and part personal reminder. Designed to maintain a tangible link with the present, it combines micro-interactions to soothe, stimulate and anchor. In the background, the device picks up WiFi signals from its immediate environment to interpret human presence and the dynamics of the place, adapting its behaviour accordingly.

It's a physical reminder of our existence in a digital and changing world.
