Tap Seeker

ux - ui, visual identity

2023

The fictitious Tap Seeker application takes a different look at the way we consume images, by dissecting and raising awareness of the stages involved in their discovery.

The processes are based on the ‘anti-user-frendly’ function, incorporating the blocking and activation of feeds, the masking and revelation of images one by one, and the imposition of waiting times.
