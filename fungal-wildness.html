Fungal Wildness

3D graphics

2024

Creation of a 3D modelling poster of an insect whose appearance has been influenced by its environment, in this case "fungal wildness".

The base is a dragonfly, eaten away by a fungus gnat. The result of a week-long workshop led by artist Kushagra Gupta.
