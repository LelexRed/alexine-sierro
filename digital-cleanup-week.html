Digital Cleanup Week

Digital Cleanup Week is a visual communication campaign selected and distributed for Digital Cleanup Week 2024, aimed at ECAL, HETS and HES-SO-Valais.

The project consists of a trio of animated posters and a printed poster in F4 format.
