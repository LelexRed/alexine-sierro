Alexine Sierro is an interactive media designer based in Vevey (CH), graduated from ECAL. Her multidisciplinary practice encompasses 3D, interactive installations, virtual reality, motion design, and visual productions of all kinds. She has a particular affinity for 3D, exploring its atmospheres and techniques in depth.

Combining reflection and experimentation, she develops work that is conceptually rigorous and bridges form and idea.
